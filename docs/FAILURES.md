# What failed and how it was caught

| Observed failure | What exposed it | Response and remaining limit |
|---|---|---|
| Oracle labeled annual revenue as a three-month amount in FY2018 through FY2022. | The direct Q4 value equaled the full-year fact. In FY2018, reported $39.383B exceeded the derived Q4 value of $11.014B. | Reject that direct quarter, use annual minus nine months and retain the warning. [Validation report](../data/processed/validation_report.md) |
| Amazon's 10-Qs included trailing-twelve-month facts. | A twelve-month duration alone could identify a rolling window as a fiscal year. | Require a 10-K or a matching fiscal-year start from YTD facts. [Quarter derivation](../src/capex_tracker/quarters.py) |
| The 7B local model timed out loading on 8 GB. | It produced no first dev response. | The original run used 1.5B. The newer dev bake-off measured four models at or below about 4B, one at a time. [Initial run](history/INITIAL_GUIDANCE_RUN.md) · [Bake-off](../eval/MODEL_BAKEOFF.md) |
| The 1.5B extractor got zero held-out dollar bounds right (0.0%, n=9). | On Meta's 2024 10-K, the retry returned $3.0B to $3.7B for a filed $30B to $37B. The response also lacked a year. | Reject on verified fields, retain both attempts and select rules under the frozen policy. [Trace](../data/guidance/traces/META-6e73e0ba7afe.json) · [Test](../eval/test_results.json) |
| Dual-extractor agreement yielded zero approved passages out of 156. | The ledger and empty comparison chart showed the consequence of requiring the weak model's agreement. | Register a separate two-document approval path before applying it. Both sources must pass verification. [Policy](decisions/ADR-005-corroboration-approval.md) |
| Six of 13 correctly detected held-out rules positives failed verification. | Every failure concerned the comparison series. One Oracle filing explicitly identified cash used for capex. Amazon's net proceeds and Alphabet's lease treatment remained unresolved. | Accept Oracle's explicit MD&A definition. The diagnostic moved from 7/13 to 8/13 verified. The published frozen scores retain the original result. [Case-by-case diagnosis](../eval/VERIFICATION_DIAGNOSIS.md) |
| Meta's combined 2024 and 2025 outlook lost its dollar target year. | One sentence gave a 2024 range followed by a separate directional expectation for 2025. | Stop the numeric clause at the second outlook and preserve the whole quote. A sentence with two ranges and two years still needs human interpretation. [Diagnosis](../eval/VERIFICATION_DIAGNOSIS.md) |
| The quarterly writer failed grounding twice. | It combined two time comparisons into an unsupported sentence. Both failures and prompts were retained. | Check each planned comparison and publish a labeled, checked template if local rephrasing fails. [Attempts](../data/guidance/draft.json) · [Tests](../tests/test_guidance_ground.py) |
| A verified Llama dev proposal classified depreciation growth as capex growth. | Gold labels and per-passage results disagreed, despite the proposal passing the original checks. | The benchmark reports the false positive. Model-only proposals cannot approve. Exact-quote checks alone cannot establish semantic scope. [Bake-off](../eval/MODEL_BAKEOFF.md) |
| Phi labeled an increase in a dollar range's high end as directional guidance. | A dev control cleared its confidence and quote gates. | Screen known numeric queue items out of the optional job and keep all model-assisted suggestions pending. No directional recall improvement was measured on four cases. [Directional evaluation](../eval/DIRECTIONAL.md) |
